PART 2 • DATA & EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE

USER RESEARCH 

01

Parents need a convenient way to track their child’s development

International

A study of 60 parents and expectant parents in the US and China documented strong demand for tracking infant feeding, sleep, and physical growth.

JMIR, 2019
Vietnam

A BeBo survey of 49 parents with children under 5 (primarily in Hanoi) revealed high demand for an app to support tracking their child's developmental milestones.

BeBo, 2023

02

Parents need to manage and receive reminders about childcare schedules

Vietnam

Surveyed parents in the BeBo study strongly desired advance event notifications and timely reminders. In Ben Tre, 93.3% of interviewees expressed willingness to pay for SMS vaccination reminders.

BeBo, 2023; Ben Tre Study, 2017
psychology Insight

Having one place to track and receive reminders for vaccinations and regular check-ups helps parents take a more proactive approach to their child's health and reduces the risk of missing important milestones.

BeBo, 2023; Ben Tre Study, 2017

03

Parents want to record milestones and special moments of their child

International

JMIR research found that parents actively use digital applications to capture developmental milestones and memorable moments in their child’s growth journey.

JMIR, 2019
psychology Insight

Saving photos and audio of a child's firsts delivers high emotional value and strengthens bonding.

JMIR, 2019
