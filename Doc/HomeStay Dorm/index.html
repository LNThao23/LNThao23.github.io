02 · Empathize & Define

User Persona & Empathy

Basic background

University student living away from home and balancing study with a limited budget.

Motivation & needs

Wants safety, transparent costs, convenient facilities and supportive roommates.

Frustrations

Misleading photos, hidden fees, delayed replies and complicated contracts.

Goal & behavior

Compares multiple options, asks friends and needs proof before making a decision.

4-Quadrant Empathy Map

Says

“I need a room that is affordable, clean and close enough to campus.”

Thinks

“Can I trust these photos, and are there any costs they are not showing?”

Does

Checks many listings, asks other students and contacts landlords directly.

Feels

Overwhelmed by scattered information but excited to find an independent home.

Proposed Product Solutions

Space Experience & Spatial Verification

  • Verified room photographs and amenity details
  • Transparent costs and policies
  • Guided comparison between suitable rooms

Practical Trust & Contract Guarantee

  • Clear digital contract journey
  • Secure booking and checkout
  • Support throughout the resident lifecycle
