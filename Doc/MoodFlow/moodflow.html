02 / Customer Persona

Understand Users

“Users do not lack advice, they lack a safe, reliable space to connect emotions and understand themselves.”

User Interview

To uncover the unspoken realities of youth mental health and emotional awareness, we conducted qualitative one-on-one deep-dive sessions with university students. The study focused on dissecting personal coping mechanisms, daily stress triggers, and their barriers to using conventional wellness solutions.

Key research parameters

  • Participants: 9 university students and young adults (Gen Z) across Hanoi & Ho Chi Minh City experiencing daily academic pressure.
  • Interview Format: Hybrid format — 1-on-1 in-person conversations and guided online video sessions creating a comfortable, judgment-free space.
  • Interview Plan & Duration: 18 open-ended qualitative questions structured around emotional triggers, self-talk habits, and coping cycles (~15 minutes per participant).

Summary of core interview findings

Participants overwhelmingly revealed that while they frequently feel overwhelmed, they struggle to pinpoint the root causes of their burnout. Most rely on passive escapism (social media scrolling, binge-watching) which heightens guilt. They strongly expressed the need for gentle, non-judgmental guidance and self-talk tools over authoritative advice.

The survey results outline a concerning reality: Users are being overwhelmed by negative emotions, but only know how to rely on passive relief habits. However, the majority admit that these coping methods are completely ineffective, leaving them stuck in a stress loop.

✦✦ 03 / Orientation and Proposed Solutions

How Might We & Proposed Solutions

HMW 1

How might we turn the journey of self-discovery into a gentler and more healing experience?

  • The app provides cute emotion emojis for users to choose as their mood for that day instead of immediately writing about their feelings.
  • The Daily Challenge feature offers fun, short challenges for users to complete based on user preferences.
  • In the Journal feature, the app provides 2 modes: Self-talk and Chat (with AI) for users to choose according to their habits and personal needs.
HMW 2

How might we help users realize the behaviors/habits that they should improve?

  • The View Statistics function in Mood Tracking allows users to review their personal mood data and sleep indicators.
  • During Chatting/Journaling, users can track their own emotions and health, thereby making appropriate adjustments to improve their mood and lifestyle habits.
