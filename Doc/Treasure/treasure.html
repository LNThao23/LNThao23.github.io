Why Was "Treasure" Created?

Born from a deep understanding of the invisible barriers children with hearing and speech impairments face daily,
the authors wished to craft an art piece that nurtures empathy right from the first pages.

INTRODUCTION

  • Children with hearing and speech impairments need alternative communication methods.
  • Empathy and an inclusive environment are the keys to connection.

CONTEXT RESEARCH

  • Non-verbal communication (eye contact, gestures) and sensory experiences are powerful connection tools.
  • Shared play activities create bonds that overcome language barriers.

RELEVANCE TO THE STORY

  • Tít (a girl who cannot hear or speak) and Hải build a deep friendship through gestures and actions on the beach.
  • Message: The greatest "treasure" is a sharing heart and understanding without the need for words.

CHARACTER DESIGN

Two characters, two distinct worlds, sharing a warm heart and a longing for connection.

Tít
Coastal girl of Quy Nhơn Non-verbal communication
  • Profile: A gentle and observant young girl living in the coastal area of Quy Nhơn. She cannot hear or speak, communicating instead through warm gestures, expressive glances, and genuine facial expressions.
  • Personality: Perceptive, peaceful, and deeply appreciative of nature. She seeks to connect with the world around her and finds joy in discovering simple, everyday beauties like sea shells and sunbeams.
  • Symbolism: She represents the innate power of non-verbal empathy and the wondrous ability to build deep, meaningful bonds far beyond spoken language.
City boy Summer holiday
  • Profile: A protective and sentimental city boy who is visiting the beach for summer vacation. He deeply values cherished memories, photo keepsakes, and honest emotional bonds with new companions.
  • Personality: Compassionate, caring, and warm-hearted. He is motivated by a genuine desire to comfort, share with, and care for those who experience pain or hardship.
  • Symbolism: He represents proactive empathy, open-mindedness, and the touching realization that true "treasure" lies in human connection and kindness rather than material possessions.
Hải
