The Red Scarf

A heartfelt tribute to resilient children

A touching picture book project created by Lê Nguyên Thảo, dedicated to children with disabilities in Vietnam. Inspired by the resilient land of Quảng Trị and the inspiring courage of little sprouts rising above the lingering wounds of war.

Historical & social research

Background & Human Inspiration

Introduction

  • Quảng Trị remains deeply affected by unexploded bombs and landmines left after the war.
  • Explosive remnants can still be found in farms, forests, rivers and neighbourhoods.
  • Safety education and clearance work remain essential for local communities.

Context Research

  • Farmers and children are among the most vulnerable groups.
  • Accidents can lead to lifelong disability, trauma and financial hardship.
  • These hidden dangers also slow local development.

Relevance to the Story

  • Gạo's journey is inspired by the resilience of children in Quảng Trị.
  • His determination celebrates courage, hope and the value of peace.
  • The book invites readers to see strength beyond every scar.
